Rules

  • Losers draw for teams at the beginning of the season and retain the same team throughout.
  • Teams available to be drawn are the "losers" of the previous season up to and including Wild Card losing teams, teams outside of the grouping are considered "winners" and are ineligible for the current LFL season.
  • The winning Loser is determined by the fewest losses during the 18-week NFL regular season.
  • Payoff amount is the sum of the difference of losses between the loser and the winning loser where each loss is $2.
  • Weekly ties are worth $0.
2026 Rule Change: Overall LFL ties are no longer allowed. If two or more teams are tied at the end of the NFL regular season, those teams will continue into the playoffs until a single LFL winner is determined, up to and including the Super Bowl.
